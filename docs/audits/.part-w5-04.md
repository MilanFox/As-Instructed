## w5-04 — Load Balance
**Verdict:** fixed

**Findings:**

1. **Seed 1 (and 2, 4, 5) passed a wrong general rule — the w3-01 defect shape, on the star.**
   The level's stated lesson is "item order decides whether greedy works". Before this pass, only
   seed 3 — the hand-constructed one — actually depended on the order. Cabling consumers in the
   order they were read (no sort at all) satisfied both objectives *and* the `largest-idle` star
   on seeds 1, 2, 4 and 5; only seed 3 refused it. A player who starts on seed 1, never sorts, and
   is rewarded with two objectives and a star learns "first fit in read order" as the general rule,
   then hits a wall on seed 3 with nothing in the brief or facts to tell them what changed. That is
   DESIGN §11.5 / CURRICULUM §15.3 verbatim.
   Measured before the fix (first-fit in world order over the feeders that must carry the load):
   seed 1 slack 1.25 → passes, seed 2 1.12 → passes, seed 4 1.08 → passes, seed 5 1.08 → passes.

2. **The premise that order decides the fit lived only in the hints.**
   Hints 2 and 3 carried it ("the awkward consumers are the big ones…", "the same set of consumers
   packs or does not pack depending only on the order"). The brief said "Put every consumer on a
   feeder. Take no feeder over its ceiling" and the facts defined `capacity`, `draw`, the
   permanence of cable and what "over its ceiling" means — nothing said a consumer can strand while
   the totals still look comfortable. DESIGN §11.3: a hint sharpens, it does not introduce. The
   graded quantities themselves (the ceiling, what a feeder's load is, what "exactly one feeder"
   means) were all stated correctly — this was the one missing premise.

3. **Hint budget spent twice on one idea (CURRICULUM §2 rule 11).** Hints 2 and 3 were both
   "take the big ones first"; the star — the only place the reduced feeder set appears — had no
   hint at all.

**Not defects (checked and clean):**

**For the user:**

1. **Par 40 is loose and has been since before this pass — I did not touch it (report-only).**
   CURRICULUM §2 rule 4 wants par = reference ticks − ~10% from the *median* seed. The reference
   lays one 2-tick cable per consumer and nothing else, so its clock is exactly 2 × consumers: median
   seed is 16 consumers → 32 ticks → par should be ~29. Par is 40, i.e. above the reference on every
   seed, so the tick target cannot be missed by anyone who solves the level. It was equally loose
   before (median 17 → 34). The header comment ("40 ticks on the twenty-consumer seed") describes a
   seed that no longer exists in the list — no draw reaches 20 consumers now. Recommend par 29 and a
   corrected comment; both are yours to take.

2. **The objectives are still order-insensitive on the drawn seeds; only seed 3 and the star carry
   the lesson.** I could not fix this from the generator. The structure hands the player one spare
   feeder that is by construction *larger than every other*, so first-fit in read order essentially
   never overflows the full set — I searched slack 1.00–1.25 across 300 draws per seed and found one
   qualifying draw in 1,200. Tightening far enough to break it would make the star (which needs the
   remaining feeders to hold the whole load) impossible. So on seeds 1/2/4/5 an unsorted run still
   clears "assigned" and "within-capacity" and loses only the star. Two ways out, both changes to
   what the level asks and therefore yours: (a) add a second constructed seed in seed 3's mould, so
   two of five seeds refuse an unsorted run outright; or (b) fold the "leave the largest cold"
   condition into the objectives and give the star to something else. My recommendation is (a) — it
   is additive and leaves the star where it is.

3. **`World.vars` on this level (`capacity` and `load` totals, `w5-04.ts:284-285`) is inert.** No
   player API reads world vars and no UI surfaces them, so those two numbers are neither hidden
   information nor available information — they are dead. Harmless; delete or expose, your call.
